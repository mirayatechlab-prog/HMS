# Indian Healthcare Compliance Requirements Register

> [!WARNING]
> **Disclaimer**: This register documents regulatory areas identified for MediCore HMS. **No legal compliance, accreditation, or regulatory certification is claimed.** All items require review by qualified Indian healthcare legal counsel, Chartered Accountants, and hospital compliance officers.

| ID | Domain | Applicable Framework / Authority | Official Source URL | Verified Date | Applicability & Engineering Impact | Outstanding Questions / Professional Review |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| COMP-01 | Patient Privacy & Digital Personal Data | Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 & Rules (MeitY) | https://www.meity.gov.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Consent notice, purpose limitation, data minimization, breach notification readiness, retention/erasure workflows (subject to medical record retention laws). | Confirm DPDP Rules commencement dates & hospital-specific consent templates with legal counsel. |
| COMP-02 | Clinical Establishment Registration | Gujarat Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act / State Health Dept | https://gujhealth.gujarat.gov.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Hospital registration metadata, sanctioned/licensed bed capacity tracking separate from operational beds, minimum standards record-keeping. | Confirm hospital's exact Gujarat registration category & reporting formats. |
| COMP-03 | Medical Documentation & Retention | National Medical Commission (NMC) Registered Medical Practitioner Regulations | https://www.nmc.org.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Standardized prescription elements, doctor registration number on Rx, immutable signed clinical notes with timestamped amendments, 3+ year indoor record retention. | Confirm specialty-specific retention durations (e.g., medico-legal / pediatric). |
| COMP-04 | Pharmacy Licensing & Restricted Drugs | Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 & Rules (CDSCO) | https://cdsco.gov.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Batch-level tracking, expiry blocking, Schedule H/H1/X restricted medicine dispensing register, pharmacist license metadata. | Confirm exact Schedule H1 register export format required by local Drug Inspector. |
| COMP-05 | Financial Invoicing & GST | Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC) — GST | https://www.cbic.gov.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Configurable tax treatment (healthcare services exemption vs taxable pharmacy/consumables), sequential tenant-scoped invoice numbering, credit notes. | Requires CA review of hospital's GST registration & HSN/SAC mappings (SQ-02). |
| COMP-06 | Biomedical Waste Records | Biomedical Waste Management Rules, 2016 (CPCB) | https://cpcb.nic.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Optional/future tracking of waste handover logs if hospital tracks it alongside external lab/ward ops. | Confirm whether hospital uses external CBWTF portal directly or needs HMS log. |
| COMP-07 | Patient Consent & Disclosures | Indian Contract Act / DPDP / Clinical Ethics | https://www.mohfw.gov.in/ | 2026-11-27 | Documented consent for admission, procedures, and authorized third-party disclosures. | Legal review of digital vs wet-ink consent workflows. |
