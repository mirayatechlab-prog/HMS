# MVP Development Roadmap (Canonical Sequence — Reconciled in Phase 0A)

- **Phase 0**: Product Architecture and Engineering Foundation.
- **Phase 0A**: Independent Review Corrections, Foundation Hardening and Final Acceptance.
- **Phase 1**: Authentication, Hospital Onboarding, Dynamic Infrastructure, Staff and Doctor Management (M01, M02, M03, M04).
- **Phase 2**: Patient Registration, OPD, Clinical Consultation and Prescriptions (M05, M06, M07).
- **Phase 3**: IPD, Bed Allocation, Nursing and Discharge Workflows (M08, M09, M14 clinical/bed release).
- **Phase 4**: Pharmacy and Inventory (M10).
- **Phase 5**: External Laboratory and Sample Collection (M11).
- **Phase 6**: Billing, Payments, Financial Operations and Settlements (M12, M13, M14 financial clearance, M15 cash reconciliation). *Note: Billing integration contracts and `Decimal(14,2)` invariants are enforced from Phase 1 onward.*
- **Phase 7**: Dashboard, Expenses and Operational Reporting (M15 expenses, M16).
- **Phase 8**: Security Hardening, UAT and Production Release (M17 hardening, penetration testing, backup/restore drills, production launch).
